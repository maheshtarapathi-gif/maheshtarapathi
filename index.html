About Me
THE STORY
BEHIND THE SCREEN

Hey, I'm Mahesh Tarapathi — a 24-year-old digital creator from Kundapura, now living and creating in Bangalore. I turned my passion for cricket and storytelling into a full-time creative journey.

I create content across Instagram, X (Twitter), and Threads — from deep cricket analysis and IPL breakdowns to portrait photography and lifestyle trends. Every piece of content I make is driven by one thing: genuine passion.

As a Reel and YouTube Editor, I help creators bring their vision to life through sharp, engaging edits that actually get views. I understand the algorithm — and more importantly, I understand what makes people stop scrolling.
